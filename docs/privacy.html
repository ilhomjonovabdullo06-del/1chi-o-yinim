Boks - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026.

Boks does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or third-party SDKs.

On-device data: the app saves your settings (controller layout, difficulty, optional rule) in a small file in the app's private storage. It never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Network (2-phone play): in "Online" mode, two phones on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot talk directly to each other over UDP. Only a randomly generated nickname (e.g. "BOKSCHI42") and gameplay input/state are exchanged between the two devices. Nothing is sent to any internet server, and we do not receive it.

Children: the app is not directed to children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
